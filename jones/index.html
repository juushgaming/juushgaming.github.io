Cats

Grumpy Cat

1 hit point · Enemy

Patrols its patch of ground. Direct contact is dangerous; it avoids walking off ledges. COUNTERPLAY Jump over it, strike with your sword or push it back with your shield. Landing on its head defeats it.

Keyboard Cat

2 hit points · Enemy

Alternates between flying notes and a low bass wave. Its playing animation announces the attack. It takes two hits to defeat. COUNTERPLAY Dodge notes or block them from the front; jump over the bass wave. Attack during the pause. Walls block its view.

Cats

Coffee Table Cat

1 hit point · Enemy

Floats high above Jones on a small coffee table. With a swipe of its paw, it pushes mugs, glasses and bottles off the edge. The landing position is locked when it winds up and marked by a golden strip. COUNTERPLAY Move out of the marked spot or face the throw to block the falling vessel. Keep moving beneath the table. You never have to jump up and defeat this high enemy to continue. It pauses after each throw.

Cats

Christmas Tree Cat

1 hit point · Enemy

Tugs on a decorated tree, then topples it towards the marked side after a 1.2-second warning. Its impact damages a broad area on that side. A hit deals one damage and shocks Jones for 0.55 seconds, shown by circling stars. COUNTERPLAY Leave the marked area, jump in time or face the tree's base and block. A successful block prevents damage and shock, costing one shield durability. The tree hits only once and disappears; the cat cannot replace it. Defeat the cat by landing on its head.

Cats

Scuba Cat

1 hit point · Underwater enemy

Patrols underwater caves in a diving suit. When Jones is visible and its attack is ready, it lunges immediately, without a windup or warning colour. The direction stays fixed during the lunge, followed by a recovery pause. COUNTERPLAY Swim sideways or upwards, block or use your sword underwater. A hit does not send Jones back to the cave entrance. You cannot stomp enemies while swimming.

White Hand Sentry

Siege ring · Stealth enemy

A dark helmeted cat bearing a white hand. Its awareness rises when Jones stands in front of it without cover. At full awareness it fires arrows. COUNTERPLAY Crouch beneath marked cover and sneak through the siege ring. Distance and blocked sight reduce awareness. This is a stealth sentry, not an ordinary cat with hit points.

Companions

Ring Guardian

Joins automatically · 1–3 cats

An armoured ring guardian whose attacks flash with blue light. COMPANIONSHIP Approach the guest to recruit them; no action button is needed. They follow with their own walking, jumping, climbing or swimming poses and attack reachable enemies independently. A random total of one to three cats defeated near Jones ends their journey. They recover for about three seconds after an attack. BASE DEFENCE Before joining, the guest defends their home against nearby cats in clear sight. These victories consume neither their future allowance nor that of an active companion. MUSIC Has an original short companion theme.

Story

Jones

Guinea pig · Messenger from the besieged castle

Jones sneaks out of Carrot Keep, crosses the siege ring and seeks help from the allied kingdom. You control him directly; he does not run automatically. DISTANCE Only the furthest point reached counts. Backtracking and crypt trips do not inflate the score. The menu shows the last run and your personal best.

Story

Sir Squeakalot

Quest giver at Carrot Keep

The herald explains Jones' mission and the sally port. His request begins the journey through the siege. The escape only counts as complete after Jones has crossed the entire siege ring.

Background

Castle Archers

Four guinea pigs · Wall defence

Defend the battlements with loading, aiming and firing animations. Their arrows strike approaching helmeted cats and produce small impact clouds. ROLE These defenders bring the siege to life. Their arrows cannot hit Jones. They cannot be recruited as companions.

Wall Assault Cats

Helmeted cats · Assault on the battlements

Advance towards the archers and are driven back by their arrows. These attackers belong to the background siege scene and do not count as cats defeated by Jones.

Siege Column

White Hand cats · Camp and siege ring

Marches behind the playable ground between siege engines, showing the scale of the attack. These figures cannot collide with Jones. The sentries in the foreground, however, can detect him.

Background

Meadow Cavalry

Ten guinea pig riders · Reinforcements

After the call for help, the mounted company charges a cat troop. A comic dust cloud marks the clash. After winning, the cavalry advances left towards home. ROLE The battle stays at a fixed site behind the action. Jones runs past it; the battle never follows him or the camera. Riders do not attack foreground enemies or consume companion allowances.

White Hand Company

Helmeted cats · Background battles

Opposes the Meadow Cavalry or a company of infantry and archers. After the clash, the cats are driven back and flee. Their background battle affects neither Jones' health nor his personal cat defeat count.

Meadow Infantry

Armoured guinea pigs · Local defenders

This small infantry company walks and fights with its own animations against startled cats or White Hand troops. Archers cover it from the rear. The battle stays at a fixed site and ends after victory. It can take place before Jones calls for help. It causes no collisions, damage or companion kills.

Field Archers

Guinea pigs · Infantry support

Hold position behind the infantry, draw their bows and fire visible volleys into the background battle. Their arrows belong only to the scene and cannot hit Jones or playable enemies. These companies occupy varied sites determined by the world seed and never follow Jones.

Equipment

Sword

Durability: 24 connected hits

On Android, the equipped sword automatically attacks reachable enemies in front of Jones. On PC, use E. A connected strike consumes durability; a miss does not. Blocking interrupts the attack. Finding another sword repairs a weapon you still carry.

Link to this entry
Equipment

Shield

Durability: 16 blocked hits

Hold the shield button and face the danger. A successful block costs one durability point; the final block still protects Jones. Walking backwards does not turn a raised shield away from the enemy.

Link to this entry
Equipment

Armour

Absorbs 3 hits

Visible armour worn by Jones absorbs hits before hearts are lost. After three hits the protection is exhausted. Spinach can repair the armour.

Link to this entry
Equipment

Elven Equipment

Crypt treasure · Greater durability

Elven blade: 48 hits. Elven shield: 32 blocks. There is a 25% chance of finding the triple-durability version, with 72 hits and 48 blocks respectively. Elven armour always absorbs nine hits and has its own visible movement poses. REPAIRS A normal pickup repairs an elven item still in use without downgrading it. Once that item breaks, an ordinary replacement has normal durability again.

Equipment

Runic Keys

Three keys open an ancient tomb

Collect three runic keys and approach an old tombstone. Your first entry consumes three keys. An unlocked entrance can be used again, but its treasure can only be claimed once.

Equipment

Carrot, Spinach, Parsley, Cucumber

Healing · Repairs · Movement · Breath

Carrots restore one heart. Spinach repairs armour; parsley temporarily improves movement. The magical cucumber temporarily lets Jones breathe underwater. The remaining duration is shown in the game.

World

Oathbound Crypts

Rare secret areas

Behind old tombstones lie six to nine randomly arranged chambers with seal galleries, water, pendulums and spectral guardians. Open every seal and defeat every guardian to claim the single elven treasure. Portals return Jones safely. Crypt travel does not increase the scored run distance.

Link to this entry
World

Underwater Caves

Mandatory diving sections

These caves lie on the main route. Air bubbles replenish the breath meter; air pockets and the magical cucumber help with long dives. Different cave shapes, currents, scuba cats and underwater guests vary the journey.

Link to this entry
World

Crawl Passages

Golden arrows · Lit ground line

Sally ports, camp cover and hollow logs mark their low passages. On mobile, hold the stick down and sideways; on PC, hold S or Down plus a movement direction. Nearby cover becomes translucent so you can still see Jones.

Story

The Seven Collars

Signs along the path

The seven original cat species guard fragments of an ancient sign. Pia seems to test Jones with her tomatoes. The coffee-table and Christmas-tree cats are additional path enemies; the old title still refers to the original seven.

Story

The Forgotten Knight

Armour and ancient signs

Stone tablets tell of a small knight who once sealed the endless loop. His armour seems to recognise Jones. Whether it once belonged to Jones or has simply been waiting for him remains part of the mystery.
