Privacy Policy – Jones the Guinea Pig

Last updated: September 30, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the game Jones the Guinea Pig, developed and published by JuushGaming.

Data Collection

Jones the Guinea Pig does not collect or transmit personal or sensitive user data to JuushGaming. The game does not require a user account and does not use advertising, analytics, tracking services, or integrated cloud services.

The game does not sell or share user data with third parties.

Local Game Data

Game information such as the local high score, previous run distance, discoveries, language choice, and game settings may be stored locally on the user's device.

The game itself does not transmit this information to JuushGaming or any third party. Your operating system may back up or restore app data according to your device and backup settings; those system services are managed by your device or platform provider.

Third Parties and External Links

Jones the Guinea Pig does not contain third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking services that collect user data.

If you choose to open the privacy policy or another external link, the game opens your web browser. Visiting that website creates a connection to its hosting provider and is subject to the provider's privacy practices. The game does not attach your scores, settings, or other game data to these links.

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement for information about GitHub's handling of website visitor data.

Data Retention and Deletion

JuushGaming does not receive or retain personal user data from gameplay in Jones the Guinea Pig.

Locally stored game data can be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the game from the device. If your system keeps a backup, manage or delete that backup through your device or platform provider's settings.

If you contact us by email, we use your email address and message to respond to your request. We retain this correspondence only for as long as needed to handle your inquiry and any applicable legal obligations. You can request deletion by contacting us below.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the functionality of Jones the Guinea Pig changes. Any changes will be published on this page.
