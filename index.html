After a long time playing games, I thought: why not make one of my own?

And thus, Jones the Guinea Pig was born.

Jones the Guinea Pig

Flee from the siege and call for aid.

Your way might be long, but you don't necessarily have to travel alone. Along the way, you may find unlikely companions who will help you fight up to three cats before moving on.

Run, jump, crawl, fight and find your way through. Survive whatever the cats throw at you and see how far you can get.

Jones the Guinea Pig is currently in early alpha. Things may change, things may break, and Jones may end up somewhere he absolutely wasn't supposed to be.
